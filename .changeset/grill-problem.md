`grilling`'s design tree can now hold nodes other than decisions when a skill running alongside it says so. `grill-me` points you at `/grill-problem` when you can't yet say what the problem is and that skill is installed. Adds two beta skills in `in-progress/`: `grill-problem` (user-invoked) and `problem-modeling` (model-invoked), which grill an ill-defined problem in any domain into a `PROBLEM.md`, a glossary and recorded decisions.
